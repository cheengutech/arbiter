How it works

From BACnet
to your phone.

01
Poll BACnet devices

Arbiter runs on-site, reading present values from controllers every 60 seconds over native BACnet/IP.

02
Score against rules

Each reading is evaluated against site-specific thresholds. Events below the noise floor are suppressed.

03
Cloak the noise

97% of BMS chatter never reaches the engineer. Only alarms that clear the priority threshold are forwarded.

04
Recall to action

One SMS — device, object, value, threshold. No app. No login. No dashboard. Just the signal.
