How it works

From BACnet to your phone.

01
Poll BACnet devices
Arbiter runs on-site, reading present values from controllers every 60 seconds over native BACnet/IP.
02
Score against rules
Each reading is evaluated against site-specific threshold rules. Events below the noise floor are suppressed before they leave the building.
03
Filter the noise
Only alarms that clear the priority threshold are forwarded. 97% of BMS chatter never reaches the engineer.
04
SMS in under 30s
Actionable alerts arrive by text with device, object, value, and threshold. No app required. No login. No dashboard.
